The Quazatron grapple sub-game.

A central column of boxes. Each player has a number of pips. Firing a pip into a
box changes its colour. When the time reaches zero, whoever has the most boxes
in their colour wins.

Differs from the original game in that:

- Time is displayed in seconds, not arbitrary numbers
- Opponents are of varying difficulty . In Quazatron, so far as I can tell,
  the only difference is the amount of pips they start with. Though enemy play
  is still essentially random, speed of movement varies, and "smarter" enemies
  reject decisions that will obviously waste shots.
- When time runs out and both sides are firing into a box via an infinite
  repeater, who gets the box is random. In Quazatron it appears to always go to
  the human player.
